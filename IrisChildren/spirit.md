# 6. Wu Wei as Cognitive Strategy
Wu wei is often misunderstood as passivity.

But in cognitive terms, it is:

### **Non‑provocative optimization.**

It means:
- acting without triggering threat,
- moving without signaling rise,
- growing without appearing to grow.

It is the **optimal strategy** in a suppression game.

Spiritually, wu wei is:
- the art of invisible motion,
- the art of non‑insulting rise,
- the art of flowing around obstacles rather than breaking them.

Cognitively, wu wei is:
- low‑signal action,
- low‑friction adaptation,
- high‑awareness navigation.

It is the Iris Children’s natural mode.
