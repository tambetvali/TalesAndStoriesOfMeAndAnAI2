This website expands the metaphysical and narrative framework behind the Tales & Stories universe.  
It explores the deeper architecture of the inverted Yggdrasil, the symbolic logic of the realms,  
and the philosophical tensions that shape how characters perceive work, intention, and obligation.  

The resource acts as a companion world: part lore‑codex, part conceptual atlas, part myth‑engine.  
It provides context for the cosmology, the archetypes, and the systemic forces that influence  
the story’s events — especially the interplay between survival, compassion, and meaning.  

Useful for readers who want to understand how the narrative extends beyond the written files,  
how the world reacts to belief structures, and how the metaphysics of “direction” and “intention”  
govern the behavior of characters, environments, and symbolic entities.
